Pivox Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Pivox is a color-by-number game provided by Muhammed Emin Çağlar, an individual developer based in Türkiye. In this policy, “we” refers to the developer and “the game” refers to Pivox. Muhammed Emin Çağlar is the data controller for personal data processed for purposes determined by the developer in connection with Pivox.

This policy covers the Android game and related support communications. For questions about data or privacy, contact ganzimidagames@gmail.com. Processing performed by Google and other service providers within their own services is also governed by their policies.

2. Information processed and its purposes

On-device game records: Painted cells, ordinary painting and Time Challenge progress, remaining time and bonus allowances, Town progress, favorites, hidden artwork, images you create, downloaded content, caches, and music, sound and gameplay preferences are stored on your device. They are used to operate the game, resume your progress and apply your preferences. Not all of these records are backed up to the cloud.

Sign-in and profile: When signing in with Google Play Games, the game uses the player identifier and session information provided by the platform. Sign-in may be attempted automatically by the platform or initiated at your request. The display name, user identifier and avatar you choose in the game are stored on your device. We do not request or store your Google account password. The current version does not use a separate Supabase account sign-in. If records from earlier versions exist, contact us about requests concerning those records.

Resources and cloud saves: Account-linked resource balances and the Google Play Games save may include game currency, spell quantities, tutorial completion, daily reward status, event reward records and Time Challenge best results. These are processed to restore supported data using the same account, maintain consistency and prevent duplicate rewards. Cloud saving is not a backup of the painting progress of every artwork.

Purchases: Product identifiers, purchase status, transaction or payment evidence and entitlement information may be processed through Google Play and the purchasing infrastructure to deliver products, restore supported purchases and prevent incorrect or duplicate transactions. We do not receive your payment card number or banking password. Google Play handles payment.

Technical connection information: When downloading content, signing in or communicating with services, providers may process IP addresses, request times, technical application or device information and service logs. These are used to establish connections, provide the service, maintain security and investigate errors. The game does not offer a feature that determines precise location using GPS. Advertising providers may infer approximate location from an IP address.

Support: When you email us, we process your email address, message and any screenshots or technical information you include to respond to your request. Do not include passwords, payment card details or unnecessary personal data.

3. Camera, photos and artwork you create

If you choose to create an image using the camera or gallery, the camera image permitted by the operating system or the photo you select is converted into pixel artwork on your device. This feature does not include a sharing flow that uploads the photo or resulting artwork to the developer’s server. You can manage camera and photo permissions in your device settings. If you refuse permission, the relevant feature may be unavailable; other painting features are independent of these permissions.

The Download button saves an exported image to your device. The Share button prepares an image file and opens the system sharing menu. You choose the application and recipient. How the receiving service processes the file is governed by that service’s rules. Copies saved to your device gallery or sent to another service are not deleted when you reset progress within the game.

4. Advertising

Pivox may display banner, interstitial or optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, ad views and clicks, application interactions and diagnostic data to deliver and measure advertisements, maintain security and prevent fraud. The information processed depends on the SDK version, device settings, region and advertising configuration. Personalized advertising is also subject to applicable consent requirements.

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to provide advertising disclosures and preference forms in regions where required. When the system requires a way to reopen preferences, an advertising privacy preferences section appears in Settings. Its visibility is determined by the requirement status reported by the provider, rather than by our own country estimate. Availability depends on the application’s AdMob configuration. You can also manage your advertising identifier and related preferences through Android’s advertising privacy settings. Changing these preferences does not remove all advertisements. The scope of any ad-free product is determined by its description on the purchase screen; optional rewarded advertisements may still be offered separately.

This policy does not constitute consent to personalized advertising or other optional processing. Where applicable law requires a separate permission or preference mechanism, displaying this policy alone is insufficient.

Google’s disclosures: Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

5. Who information is shared with

Google Play / Play Games: For authentication, supported cloud saves, purchases, entitlements and platform services.

Google AdMob and advertising service providers: For advertisement delivery, measurement and security. The scope depends on the advertising configuration and the providers disclosed by Google.

Supabase: In the current version, for serving the game catalog and content files. The provider may process technical logs relating to these connections. Any records associated with account or profile services from earlier versions are addressed separately. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.

Competent authorities or advisers, where necessary: To comply with a valid legal obligation, protect rights or handle a dispute, limited to the information necessary for that purpose.

These services may use infrastructure outside Türkiye, and your data may therefore be processed abroad. The legal conditions for international transfers depend on the data, provider, recipient country and applicable law. This policy does not replace required transfer safeguards or separate explicit consent.

6. Collection methods and legal grounds

Data is obtained through the game, device storage, service provider SDKs, secure network connections, account services and support email, using automated means or, for support communications, partly automated means.

For identity, profile and transaction information necessary to provide requested sign-in or profile features, game saves, resource synchronization and purchased products, the legal ground is that processing is directly related to entering into or performing a contract (Article 5(2)(c) of Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, or KVKK). Processing service or transaction information in a support request also relates to providing the service you request. Retaining evidence concerning a dispute is based on establishing, exercising or protecting a right (Article 5(2)(e)).

Technical logs necessary for connection security, abuse prevention and investigating technical errors are assessed on the basis of legitimate interests, provided that your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed (Article 5(2)(f)). Legally mandatory processing, such as responding to legally valid requests from competent authorities, is based on the relevant legal obligation (Article 5(2)(ç)).

Where consent is legally required for personalized advertising or non-essential tracking, the legal ground is the separate explicit consent required for that processing (Article 5(1)). An operating system camera or photo permission is not, by itself, consent to processing for other purposes. In regions subject to other rules, such as the European Economic Area, grounds including contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests and consent are assessed under the applicable law. This policy does not request blanket consent for all processing.

7. Retention, resetting and deletion

Local data may remain on your device while you use the relevant feature. Uninstalling the game or clearing its Android application data removes records in the application’s private storage. Images exported to your gallery and platform or cloud records remain separate. Operating system, device and store behavior may vary.

Resetting an artwork through its Trash menu clears ordinary painting progress; it does not delete the artwork from the catalog or its separate Time Challenge save. A reset or sign-out action in Settings is not an account deletion request unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Play Games cloud information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide supported synchronization services. Support correspondence may be retained for as long as necessary to resolve the request and protect related rights. Legal obligations, disputes and security needs may require longer retention of certain information. Deletion or anonymization of information that is no longer necessary is assessed under applicable obligations. Providers’ logs and backups may follow their own retention processes; immediate, simultaneous deletion from every backup is not guaranteed.

You may send a data or account deletion request to ganzimidagames@gmail.com with the subject “Pivox data deletion request”. Specify the scope of your request and your in-game user identifier. Do not send passwords or session tokens. Necessary and proportionate identity verification may be requested to prevent deletion of another person’s data. The “Delete My Account” option at the bottom of Settings attempts to delete and verify deletion of the Pivox cloud file belonging to the signed-in Play Games player. Internet access and a valid Play Games session are required. If successful, Pivox application records on this device are cleared and the game closes; any remaining cleanup is checked again at the next startup. In-flight synchronization is allowed to finish before deletion, and cloud synchronization for the deleted player remains disabled in this installation to prevent the old save from being recreated. A hash of the player identifier is retained on the device for this purpose. If deletion cannot be verified, success is not reported, device records are preserved and you can retry. This feature does not delete your Google account, Play Games profile, other games or store subscriptions. External copies saved to your gallery remain separate. Reinstalling and signing in again may create a new game save. You can also manage Google Play Games data through Google’s account or Play Games management tools. Deletion through Google may not automatically remove all information held in a separate developer account.

Depending on the law applicable in your country, additional rights may include access, portability, restriction of processing, objection and withdrawal of consent. Withdrawing consent does not retroactively invalidate earlier lawful processing. Send rights requests to the contact address above; they will be handled within applicable procedures and time limits. Your right to contact a competent data protection authority, including Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Authority, is reserved.

9. Age group

Pivox is designed for users aged 16 and older and does not target children under 16. Users under 18 should review this policy and the terms of use with a parent or legal representative. This statement does not mean that verified parental consent has been obtained. Where local law requires a higher age threshold or separate permission, those rules apply.

If you believe a child’s data has been processed inappropriately, contact us. After verifying the request, appropriate deletion or protective measures will be assessed.
